# Tools

NSAS accessories for everyday tools and supplies around the desk. Each part hangs on any NSAS panel with standard keys.

## Mini coffee cup holder (`mini-coffee-cup-holder/`)

Holder for one mini cup of freeze-dried instant coffee, an open ring that grips the cup by its taper, with two NSAS accessory holes. One variant per brand: Starbucks and Luckin Coffee. See [`mini-coffee-cup-holder/README.md`](mini-coffee-cup-holder/README.md).

## IKEA UPPDATERA and VARIERA - NSAS slot in holder (`ikea-uppdatera-variera-slot-in-holder/`)

Work in progress. A slot profile from a Fusion sketch (front ledge, wall leaning back 5°, overhanging lip), extruded 70 mm. See [`ikea-uppdatera-variera-slot-in-holder/README.md`](ikea-uppdatera-variera-slot-in-holder/README.md).
