壱What it is

A pegboard-style mounting surface. A grid of 5 mm square sockets is cut into a panel. Every socket slopes 45° downward into the material. Accessories such as trays and tool holders carry matching holes, and a square key joins the accessory to a socket. The key is one straight bar, so the accessory hole lies on the same 45° line as the socket but, seen from the accessory's back face, it rises as it goes in (section 7).

The socket slopes down as it goes in, so the accessory's weight pushes the key deeper. The key cannot be pulled straight out. To remove it, lift and tilt. No clips or screws are needed.

An optional thin groove between socket rows hollows out the panel and stiffens it for FDM printing.

陸Key

The key joins the accessory to the panel sockets. It is one straight 45° square bar with a thin stop plate across it. The bar fits snugly into the panel (not a press fit), so accessories can be moved and replaced quickly. It press-fits into the accessory, so it stays with the accessory when moved. The stop rests flat on the panel face and sets the insertion depth.

The two side faces are flat and flush along the whole key (4.60 apart), so it prints lying on one side. Both legs share one profile: a split peg whose upper and lower faces widen in a straight line from the root to a square tip, with a 1.00 slot along the axis that splits it into two prongs. Neither leg has a chamfer, lead-in or root relief (v0.13). Instead, every edge that runs across the key between the flat sides has a 0.50 round: the tips, slot mouths, slot ends and stop ends. The four inside corners where the stop meets the legs stay sharp, so the stop seats flat. The legs differ only in fit. The accessory leg is an expanding peg: it is 5.80 long, 0.20 short of the 6.00 hole floor, and widens from 4.80 at the root to an oversize 5.10 at the tip, so in the 5.00 hole the prongs squeeze together and spring back against the walls. The panel leg has a lighter fit: it widens from 4.60 to 5.00 (line-to-line), and its slot stops 3.00 from the panel face. The accessory slot runs back toward the stop until its end is 5.00 from the panel slot end, measured along the bar, so it ends 0.46 inside the stop (View D1). Neither leg has crush ribs (v0.11): both keep 0.20 side clearance per side.

  • Shallow faces: shorten the panel leg to that face's functional depth. Keep the 1 mm relief.
  • Print orientation: lie the key on either flat side. The slot walls are then vertical, and each prong flexes within its layers.
  • Tuning: loose: raise the tip (5.10 → 5.20). Too hard to push in: lower the tip or widen the slot. Keep the slot wider than tip − hole, so the prongs never close up.
  • Stop check: the accessory slot ends inside the stop, so the stop is thin at the slot end (0.18 at the deepest point). After a few insert-and-remove cycles, look for cracks there. If it cracks, increase the slot spacing or thicken the stop.
  • Fit check: before a full accessory, print one key and a small block with one 5.00 hole. Adjust the tip size, then keep the values that work for your printer.
  • Tip gap: keep the accessory leg 0.20 shorter than the hole depth. Zero gap fitted best in test, but then a hole printed slightly shallow stops the key before the stop seats.

漆Accessory interface

Holes go in the accessory's back face, the flat face that rests against the panel. The key's stop sits between the two faces.

Direction — common mistakeThe key is one straight bar. Its panel leg goes into the panel and down, so its accessory leg points out of the panel and up. The accessory hole follows that leg: from the back face it goes in and rises 45°, 1 mm up for every 1 mm in. Same 45° line as the socket, run the other way. Never copy the socket's "45° down into the face" onto the accessory: a downward hole cannot take a key that is already in the panel. See View D.

Gravity lock: the accessory is pressed onto the rising leg, and its weight pulls it down and back along the bar, toward the panel.

Placement

  • Horizontal hole spacing, C–C: a multiple of 10.00. Reference tray: 2 holes, 40 apart.
  • Vertical hole spacing, C–C: a multiple of 18.536.
  • Width for side-by-side hanging: n columns wide = n × 10 − clearance. Suggested clearance 1.00 total, so a 2-column holder is 19.00 wide.
  • Top margin: the floor ends one depth higher than the mouth. Top of the back face to top of the upper mouth ≥ depth + skin (6 + 2 = 8.00).
  • Wall behind the floor: check cavities, slopes and fillets inside the accessory. Keep at least 2.00 skin.
  • Check before finishing: from the back face, every hole goes in and up. A key already in a panel socket must slide straight into it.
